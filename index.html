The Problem We're Solving

India's Hidden Oil Crisis

Every year India generates an enormous volume of used cooking oil — and almost none of it is safely recovered. The cost is paid in public health and polluted water.

6.2B

Litres of used cooking oil

Generated across India every single year.

3×

Higher health risk

Reused cooking oil is linked to a sharply higher risk of cancer and heart disease.

Most of it

Never recovered

Re-enters the food chain illegally, or is dumped into drains and local water bodies.

Reused oil quietly re-enters the food chain through street kitchens.

Sengoal exists to close this gap.

The consumer loop is the moat

Households order fresh, lab-certified oil — and return the used oil in a Sengoal-provided specialised tin fitted with an IoT system that automatically measures the oil level. One loop secures a proprietary, below-market feedstock, builds a consumer brand, and generates quantifiable ESG impact.

About NAYA Foundation

On the Path of Vivekananda

Inspired by Swami Vivekananda's powerful proclamation, "Give me one hundred strong youth, and I shall build a mighty nation," the organization "Namma Assembly Youth Association", popularly known as NAYA, was born.

NAYA was formally launched on January 12, 2015, coinciding with the birth anniversary of Swami Vivekananda, with just 30 young members. Initially, it began its work at the assembly constituency level. The very first program, organized on March 15, 2015, at the Adugodi Community Hall in the BTM Assembly Constituency, saw the participation of over 500 youths, igniting a new sense of hope and enthusiasm.

Today, thousands of young people have associated themselves with NAYA and are walking the path shown by Vivekananda. What began in a single assembly constituency has now spread its roots across every assembly constituency and ward of Bengaluru. Numerous youths, driven by self-motivation, have become part of NAYA and are actively demonstrating social responsibility—this is no exaggeration.

The guiding force behind NAYA is Bharath Bushan. From his student days itself, he has nurtured a deep concern for people and for life. Today, Bharath Bushan is a well-known and respected name in the social sphere. Always among the people, actively sharing in their joys and sorrows, he carries an unwavering desire to contribute meaningfully to society. This very passion gave birth to NAYA. Even today, Bharath Bushan is regarded by many as a close friend, a relative, and a brother.

The core objective of NAYA is "Service and Organization." Through organized service, the organization continuously works to instill and spread the spirit of Vivekananda's thoughts everywhere and in everyone, making it a living, breathing mission of everyday life. That is why NAYA has grown steadily and dynamically as a platform for like-minded individuals.

NAYA's service during the COVID-19 pandemic was truly memorable—providing food, medical assistance, and financial support, and consistently responding to people's suffering.

Through initiatives such as environmental awareness, cleanliness drives, de-addiction campaigns, blood donation camps, health camps, and by commemorating Swami Vivekananda's ideals on his birth anniversary via "Viveka Jagruti Shobha Yatras" in every assembly constituency, NAYA has played a significant role in social awakening. The organization has also honored grassroots service providers—sanitation workers, ASHA workers, doctors, police personnel, anganwadi workers, and many others who serve society tirelessly. In this way, NAYA has created a new chapter of social commitment.

Today, NAYA celebrates its ten glorious years. Sustaining an organization for a full decade with continuity, commitment, and strong ideological grounding—while building a robust team of dedicated youth—is no easy task. NAYA has achieved exactly that. Not stopping there, it has taken yet another step forward by launching the NAYA Foundation, dedicated to service to the nation.

About the Founder: Bharath Bushan

Bharath Bushan is a passionate youth leader, political activist, and social worker who founded the NAYA Foundation, an NGO dedicated to empowering young people and supporting their personal, educational, and community development. With a strong belief that youth are the catalysts of positive change, Bharath started the foundation to create meaningful opportunities for learning, leadership, and service.

Under his leadership, NAYA Foundation focuses on programs that strengthen skills, confidence, and social awareness among young individuals. The foundation organizes workshops, mentorship sessions, community projects, and events that help youth discover their potential, develop life skills, and engage with real-world challenges.

Bharath's vision is to nurture a generation of socially-responsible, confident, and creative young leaders who can contribute positively to society. Through his work with the foundation, he encourages young people to step up, participate in community development, and use their talents to make a lasting impact. Driven by empathy, innovation, and a commitment to youth development, Bharath Bushan continues to inspire and mobilize young minds toward collaboration, learning, and action for a better future.
